Fraud Awareness & Candidate Safety

Overseas recruitment fraud harms genuine candidates.

We will never ask you to send money through an unofficial channel.

  • Use only our official contact channels listed on this website.
  • Verify job information directly with our office before making any payment.
  • Do not send money to unknown individuals claiming to represent this agency.
  • Do not trust unofficial social media accounts using our name or logo.
  • Verify any suspicious job offer directly with our office before responding.
  • Do not share sensitive documents unless requested through our official channels.
  • Keep payment receipts and copies of all official communication.
